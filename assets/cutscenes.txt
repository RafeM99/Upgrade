# cutscenes.txt (akshat): the boss scenes, Pokemon-style (ui/Cutscene.cpp).
# The story itself (prologue, a memory card after every upgrade, the ending album) is told by
# ui/Story with the words in strings.txt. These scenes belong only to the three bosses.
#
# FORMAT
#   [scene_name]            starts a scene. Add  vs=<boss>  for the wild-battle "VS" intro.
#   speaker: text           one line of dialogue. Long text wraps and pages by itself.
#   Speakers: gran, sam, school_bell, last_train, forgetting, narrator.
#   A line with no "speaker:" is spoken by the narrator. Lines starting with # are comments.
#
# The game plays these scene names (each once per run):
#   boss_<id>_intro    the boss appears (stage 2 school_bell, stage 4 last_train, stage 6 forgetting)
#   boss_<id>_defeat   the boss is beaten, before the world upgrades (or the ending)
# Which stages have a boss: [boss] stages in config.ini.
# Remove a scene (or all its lines) and the game just skips it.

# ---- Stage 2, 8-bit: Sam's first day at school (2006)
[boss_school_bell_intro vs=school_bell]
school_bell: DING-A-LING! Nine o'clock! Late, late, LATE! Little boys who dawdle get left behind!
gran: I know you. You rang every morning for six years, and I was never once late.
sam: I was. Every day. You carried my shoes so I'd run faster.
school_bell: Time waits for nobody, Edith! I'll ring every minute right out of you!
gran: It's Edie. And I've waited at that gate longer than you've been hanging up there. Come on, Sam.

[boss_school_bell_defeat]
school_bell: Dong... three o'clock... home time...
gran: Three o'clock. I told him I'd be right here.
sam: And you were, Gran. Every single day.

# ---- Stage 4, 32-bit: the train to the city (1964)
[boss_last_train_intro vs=last_train]
last_train: ALL ABOARD! Departing in ten seconds! Nine! Eight! Miss me, Edie, and you never meet him!
gran: Arthur's on that train. In a borrowed suit, two sizes too big.
sam: Then we're not missing it. How long have we got?
last_train: Sixty years, give or take! Seven! Six! TOOT TOOT!
gran: Sixty years is plenty. Mind the gap, Sam.

[boss_last_train_defeat]
last_train: Hsssss... this service is delayed... due to... sentiment...
gran: He stood up and gave me his seat. He said he'd get off wherever I did.
sam: And did he?
gran: Every stop, love. Every stop for sixty years.

# ---- Stage 6, full 3D: home, the oldest memory (1951). The final boss: the Forgetting itself.
[boss_forgetting_intro vs=forgetting]
forgetting: There you are, Edie. The first one. The last one. I saved it till the end.
gran: I know you. You're the one who takes the days back. You took this morning.
sam: Gran, it's after the cottage. If that goes, it all goes.
forgetting: Let go of the first one, and all the rest will follow. Every clock runs back to me.
gran: Then it can run back without me. Hold my hand, Sam. Tight.

[boss_forgetting_defeat]
forgetting: You can't keep them... not for ever...
gran: No. Not for ever. Just for now. Now is enough.
sam: The cottage is still there, Gran. Your mum's at the gate.
gran: Then let's not keep her waiting. Tea's at five.
